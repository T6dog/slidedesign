A bounded pilot is the next decision

A fictional service business will test a revised operating process in one region before deciding whether to expand it.

Proposed decision

Approve an eight-week pilot with a named owner, baseline and review gate.

Expansion depends on measurable improvement and acceptable operating cost.

What remains uncertain

Whether the observed delay comes from capacity, process variation or customer mix.

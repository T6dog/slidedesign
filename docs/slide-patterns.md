# Slide-pattern catalogue

Original structures for common business questions. Choose the structure from the evidence, then adapt it to your own identity.

| Pattern | Question it answers | Required evidence | Typical failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision summary | What should we decide and why? | Recommendation, alternatives, reason, condition | Recommendation with no explicit decision |
| Comparative bar exhibit | Where is the largest difference? | Values, common unit, population and date | Non-zero baseline or inconsistent scope |
| Time series | What changed over time? | Comparable intervals, definitions and source | Presenting forecasts as observed history |
| Contribution bridge | What explains the movement? | Opening value, additive changes, closing value | Components that do not reconcile |
| Options table | Which option best meets the criteria? | Criteria, evidence, trade-offs, uncertainty | Scores presented as objective without a rubric |
| Priority matrix | Which initiatives deserve attention? | Defined axes, scoring method and input dates | Arbitrary bubble placement |
| Roadmap | Who delivers what, and when? | Milestones, owners, dependencies, decision gates | A list of aspirations without accountable owners |
| Risk and response | What could alter the recommendation? | Trigger, impact, mitigation and residual exposure | Treating a risk list as a mitigation plan |
| Assumption register | What must remain true? | Assumption, rationale, sensitivity and validation | Hidden assumptions in headline figures |
| Source appendix | Can a reviewer reproduce the finding? | Original URL/file, version, extraction and calculation | A generic source label that cannot be traced |

The [five-page HTML starter](../templates/executive-deck.html) implements decision, comparison, options, roadmap and assumption patterns. The [brief](../templates/decision-brief.md) separates evidence gathering from rendering.

For external pattern catalogues, inspect [likaku](https://github.com/likaku/Mck-ppt-design-skill) and read [Deckary's slide-type guide](https://deckary.com/blog/pillar-slide-types-guide) at source. Their exact layouts, illustrations and brand assets have not been copied into these templates.

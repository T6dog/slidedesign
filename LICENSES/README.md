# Third-party notices

These files are byte-for-byte license and NOTICE snapshots from the reviewed upstream revisions. They remain under their original terms. Do not rewrite legal notices to match the hub's prose style.

[sources.json](../sources.json) records each upstream path, pinned revision, evidence URL and SHA-256 hash. `scripts/check-licenses.sh` compares these snapshots with the initialized submodule files at the recorded revision.

The full Apache 2.0 text is also provided as [Apache-2.0.txt](Apache-2.0.txt) because qbstyles incorporates it by reference. Trademark reservations in qbstyles remain applicable. The likaku NOTICE includes dependency attribution; bundled dependencies and any future installed packages retain their own licenses.

Keeping these notices does not clear unrelated third-party assets. Known unresolved asset/licensing cases are link-only in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md).
